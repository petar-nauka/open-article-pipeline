# Editorial method

The pipeline is a sequence of decisions, not a fixed article template:

```text
brief → source assessment → evidence ledger → draft
      → challenge → correction → final edit → delivery
```

## Why keep a ledger?

A source list says what was read. The ledger records which claim a source supports, where, with what limits, and as of when. It helps catch source laundering, stale project status, unsupported causal language, mismatched units, and headlines that exceed the body. It is an internal working document; the public article needs citations appropriate to its outlet.

## Why separate writing and checking?

The author can become attached to an angle. A separate reviewer with a clean brief can find issues the author overlooks. The handoff includes the evidence boundary, verification scope, source and tool restrictions, and access to the originals, alongside the draft, ledger, mode, and date. The author's rationale and expected verdict are excluded. When an independent reviewer is unavailable, a dedicated adversarial pass is still useful, but it should be described as a self-review. An automated validator never substitutes for inspecting evidence.

After corrections, recheck the affected claims and obtain additional support within the permitted scope when needed. Repeating the full research stage is reserved for corrections that change the central conclusion.

## Source boundaries

- **Source-only:** represent supplied material accurately. Attribute its unsupported assertions and avoid adding external facts. A fact check of that material is a separate scope.
- **Researched:** find and inspect external evidence. Seek the right source for each claim and check changing statuses for later events.
- **Opinion:** a transparent viewpoint can shape the argument, but names, dates, numbers, quotations, and factual premises still need support.

Risk depth (FAST, STANDARD, STRICT) is independent of these boundaries. One could write a strict source-only summary of a legal document without claiming to have established the document's real-world effects.

## What the checks can establish

The skills can improve provenance and expose uncertainty. They cannot prove that every source is correct, guarantee that no relevant source was missed, decide an organization's official position, or authorize publication. Readiness is an editorial judgment with a stated scope and date.

# Delivery and readiness

`READY`: the main argument and all material facts, names, dates, numbers, quotes, links, and changing statuses have sufficient inspected support; corrections were rechecked; no blocker remains. If the work required independent high-risk review, that review has actually occurred.

`READY AFTER CHECKS`: the article is otherwise complete, but a small named verification or editorial check remains. Do not bury the missing check.

`DRAFT ONLY`: a central source cannot be inspected, a consequential claim remains unresolved, an essential current-status check is missing, a quotation cannot be authenticated, or independent high-risk review is required but unavailable.

For source-only adaptations, state whether readiness refers to faithful representation of supplied material or independent verification of its factual claims.

When exporting to HTML, a CMS, Word, or PDF, first finish source checking in the working text. Then compare the export with the final source for missing paragraphs, broken links, heading hierarchy, tables, special characters, quotation marks, captions, and image credits. A clean export is a separate check from factual accuracy.

Do not add byline, publication date, organization statement, social copy, images, SEO package, or call to action unless they are supplied, requested, or inherent to the specified outlet. Do not publish or send the result externally without authority.

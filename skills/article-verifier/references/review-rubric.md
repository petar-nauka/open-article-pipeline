# Review rubric

## Scope and priority

- **Blocking:** a claim essential to the headline or conclusion; a consequential legal, medical, financial, safety, or reputational assertion; a direct quote; a number, date, role, or current status whose error changes the article.
- **Important:** a comparison, causal reading, recommendation, or context statement that materially affects interpretation.
- **Background:** stable definitions and incidental context, checked proportionately unless STRICT or explicitly exhaustive.

Split mixed statements into checkable parts. A source may support one part and not the other. Preserve negation, conditions, population, place, time, units, denominator, and whether the finding was observed, modeled, projected, or self-reported.

## Outcomes

| Label | Meaning | Editorial response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SUPPORTED | Inspected evidence directly supports wording and scope | Keep, with appropriate citation |
| PARTLY SUPPORTED | Evidence supports a narrower formulation | Qualify or narrow |
| CONTRADICTED | Reliable inspected evidence conflicts with the assertion | Correct or remove; explain conflict if material |
| UNSUPPORTED | Adequate support not found | Research more, attribute cautiously, or remove |
| CONFLICTING | Credible evidence disagrees and cannot yet be resolved | Show uncertainty or block a definitive conclusion |
| OUTDATED | Later evidence changes the reported status | Update with date and new source |
| ACCESS LIMITED | Relevant source could not be adequately inspected | Narrow, disclose, or block according to importance |

An exact primary text can be decisive for what that text says. It may not settle the effect or meaning of a contested policy. A company's milestone announcement establishes the announcement, not necessarily independent performance. Search snippets and generated summaries cannot settle a verdict.

When a quote is translated, verify the original wording and check whether the translation preserves modality and tone. Avoid long copied excerpts in the report. If source access is partial, identify the precise limitation rather than guessing.

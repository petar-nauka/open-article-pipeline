# Article writer

Read the brief and evidence ledger before committing to an angle. Select the article's core reader question and the strongest answer the evidence permits. Match the genre; read [genre guidance](references/genres.md) when the form is uncertain. Preserve requested length as a target, not a reason to add unsupported material.

Make each section advance the reader's understanding. Lead with the most relevant verified point or a sourced scene when the genre supports one. Introduce necessary context at the point of use. Keep facts, attributed claims, analysis, forecasts, and opinion distinguishable. If the brief asks for a publisher's house style, apply it without inventing an institutional position.

Link or cite material claims according to the requested format. In a working draft, optional `[C001]` markers may connect paragraphs to the ledger; remove them in the final article. Do not invent names, roles, dates, numbers, quotes, scenes, motives, outcomes, sources, or links. A direct quote needs exact source wording and context. A translation should be identified as one where relevant.

For interviews and transcripts, do not combine separate answers into a purported verbatim quote or invent a question. For an opinion article, identify the viewpoint and verify every factual premise. For a report or press release adaptation, attribute unverified claims to the source and avoid presenting a planned action as an achieved result.

After drafting, do a quick structural pass: check headline strength against evidence, section purpose, repetition, useful explanation of unfamiliar terms, and whether the conclusion follows from the body. Pass the draft and ledger to `article-verifier` before calling it final.

- [ ] Important limits are near the relevant claims.
- [ ] New or changed claims after review rechecked.

- [ ] Source list contains only sources actually used, if requested.
- [ ] Export compared with final source, if applicable.
- [ ] Readiness and remaining material checks stated outside the article.
